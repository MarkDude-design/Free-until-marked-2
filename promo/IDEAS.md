# Promo ideas — two short concepts

PUBLIC WORKING BRANCH · 2026-11-01 · Target: approximately 30 seconds each; unrecorded and untimed

These are draft words, not quotations from a past performance. They do not become main-branch promises or finished videos merely by being written.

## Clip 1 — The postcard

**Job:** introduce the talk's small encounter and leave one clear question.

**Candidate spoken copy — Meraki draft using Mark's approved opening wording:**

I want to begin with something small enough to put in your pocket. A postcard. Someone has made a picture, and someone else stops to look. It's a connection. A reaction. You can like it. You can put it down. Before the Name starts there—and asks what changes when we learn how the picture was made.

**Picture:** use the actual human postcard image when cleared, or film the physical card without revealing uncleared artwork. No extra generated promotional artwork is required.

## Clip 2 — The credit

**Job:** make the central distinction visible without dismissing the production objection.

**Candidate spoken copy — Meraki draft:**

“This is a photograph.” That names a medium. “This is good art.” Now we're judging it. “This is my kind of art.” Now you know something about me. Then we add another label: “AI-generated.” Useful information. It still leaves questions about the work, its source, and the people who contributed. A credit helps us look again. It doesn't finish the conversation.

**Picture:** ordinary text cards, speaker, and cleared existing talk material. Keep the delivery dry and let the distinction carry the clip.
