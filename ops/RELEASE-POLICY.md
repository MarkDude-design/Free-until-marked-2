# Release policy

## Main

Main is the reader/reviewer edition: CFP snapshot, complete spine, selected developed passages, current slide map, actual media records and finished promo links. A “current version” link points to main/talk/CURRENT.md; the dated CFP file remains the prepared submission snapshot.

## Workshop

The workshop branch holds the fuller public-safe performance draft and optional development. Promote a passage by reviewing its purpose, source claims and fit, then making a focused PR or commit containing only the selected material. Do not merge an entire experimental branch merely to keep one paragraph.

## Promo ideas

The promo-ideas branch holds draft short concepts, target lengths, candidate copy and review notes. When a clip is actually published, add its real URL, title, transcript/captions, credits and publication date to main/media/PROMOS.md. Review can happen next week without holding today's CFP.

Two target-30-second clips are planned on that branch. No clip has been recorded, timed or published by creating these files. Finished clips travel to main as links and associated records; an unreviewed ideas folder does not have to travel with them.

## Source handling and credit

All branches in this repository are public. Private raw rehearsals, personal-history material and uncleared third-party images remain in the private source homes. Public development copies contain cleared/generalized stage text only. Historical references and supplied assets keep their actual credits.

Words have a production history too: Mark's concepts, testimony, rehearsal wording and performance; Meraki's editorial structure and AI-written connective/draft language. Do not let a later performance erase that history.

This policy defines a manual editorial workflow; it creates no automatic watcher, scheduled review, publication or merge.
